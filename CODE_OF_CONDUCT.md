# Community code of conduct

Be considerate, specific, and respectful. Welcome people with different experience levels and backgrounds. Critique software behavior and ideas rather than a person's identity or ability. Assume good intent while taking reported harm seriously.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not share private recordings or transcripts without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful material, close disruptive conversations, or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and explained where doing so does not expose private information.

For a conduct concern, open an issue containing only a request to contact a maintainer privately. Do not include personal details publicly. For a security issue, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
